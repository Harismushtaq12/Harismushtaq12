## 🔬 Research

Click a topic to open it.

<details>
<summary><b>🎭 Multimodal deepfake detection with explainable AI</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>BS thesis</i></summary>

<br/>

My final-year thesis. It grew out of a smaller deepfake detection project (ICAT) that placed **1st in my department** and **2nd across the NUTECH School of IT**.
The goal is to detect fake media using more than one signal, and to explain *why* the model made its choice, so people can trust the result.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💎 Does AI materials discovery lean on critical elements?</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>in progress</i></summary>

<br/>

An audit of AI-proposed materials (from the GNoME dataset) compared with known materials. I check how much they depend on critical raw material elements and supply risk. I run the analysis myself in Google Colab with Materials Project data.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🕸️ Physics-informed graph neural network for critical mineral trade</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>in progress</i></summary>

<br/>

A heterogeneous graph neural network that models trade flows of critical minerals such as lithium and nickel. It adds a mass-conservation rule to the loss, so the model respects basic physics. Built step by step in Colab notebooks.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📚 Literature review: AI for critical-element-free materials</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>early stage</i></summary>

<br/>

A planned review of how AI helps replace critical elements in functional materials such as magnets, battery cathodes and catalysts.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔒 Synthetic data for privacy-compliant AI</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>co-author, in progress</i></summary>

<br/>

A paper on using synthetic data to train useful AI models while respecting privacy rules. I am working to add real experiments to it.

</details>
